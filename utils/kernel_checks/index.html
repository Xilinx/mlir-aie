Nightly Kernel Checks

Key
Every metric is smaller-is-better.
  • Charts: one per NPU, case and metric, a line per power mode, a point per nightly run. Hover for the run's commit and the spread over its calls; click a point to open the commit. The percentage beside a title is the change from the previous nightly, the worst of the modes shown: red is more than 0.5% worse, green more than 0.5% better.
  • Kernels: one row per kernel factory, and per NPU the builds it offers and how its cases fared in the latest nightly: failing cases failed the correctness sweep and are not timed, cases pass passed it, not checked on hardware has no case, and — is not offered on that NPU. Expand a kernel for its cases' latest cycles, core ELF size and NPU time, colored as in the charts; gray values are from an earlier nightly than the latest.
