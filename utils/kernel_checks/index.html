Key: metrics, thresholds and colors
  • Every metric is smaller-is-better. A change is colored red past its metric's threshold worse and green past it better; the thresholds above are shared with the pull-request report.
  • Dashboard: the latest run per NPU, its provenance, what it checked, and the series that moved past their threshold since the previous nightly in the same power mode. Warnings name anything that makes the night's numbers less comparable.
  • Kernels: one row per kernel factory, and per NPU the builds it offers and how its cases fared in the latest nightly: failing cases failed the correctness sweep and are not timed, timing failed cases passed it but failed in the timing run (their series has a gap), cases pass passed it, not checked on hardware has no case, and — is not offered on that NPU. Expand a kernel for its cases' latest numbers; passed, not timed cases are checked for correctness only, and gray values are from an earlier nightly than the latest. Hover a cycle count for the median and slowest call behind it; truncated marks one whose trace buffer filled before every call, so its min is over the calls it held.
  • Charts: one per NPU, case and metric, a line per power mode, a point per nightly run, by date. Hover for the run's commit, spread and provenance; click a point to open the commit. Dashed lines mark a change of Peano, host or driver between runs. The change sorts order charts by their latest change relative to the metric's threshold.
