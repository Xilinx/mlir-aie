DMAChannelAnalysis::BDWalkSets DMAChannelAnalysis::walkSetsThatFit ( const AIETargetModel & target,

llvm::function_ref< bool(DMAChannelDir, int)> free,
llvm::function_ref< int64_t(int)> spent,
unsigned maxSets = std::numeric_limits<unsigned>::max() )
static

Every set of walkable pools whose walk lets planBDChannels fit ends (poolOf gives each end's pool), ordered by the bytes the set walks whole, then its size, then its members in nextPoolToWalk's order; at most maxSets of them, cheapest first (unbounded by default).

Empty when the ends fit unwalked. At most 12 pools are walkable: each draws a channel of its own, and more ends than channels make channelShort. aie-objectfifo-allocate and the SA placer both walk by this; the SA only ever takes the first set, so it should pass maxSets=1.
